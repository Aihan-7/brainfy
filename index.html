06 / House rules

Five rules, in writing.

Most study apps are built to keep you in the app. Brainfy is built to a different list, and it’s short enough to type on one page. Hold us to it.

House rules

  1. No ads.

    Not between your cards, not on the timer, not in the tutor. Nobody is paying to interrupt you. sponsored deck

  2. No streak guilt.

    Your streak counts the days you studied. Every five days in a row earns a freeze for the day life gets in the way, and freezes are never for sale. buy a streak repair

  3. Honest numbers.

    Stats come straight from your own sessions: minutes focused, sessions, cards reviewed. No leaderboards, and no “top 3% of learners” badges. you beat 97% of users!

  4. Your notes stay yours.

    They sync only to your own account and are never sold. Export any deck to CSV whenever you want to leave. shared with partners

  5. Built in the open.

    Every commit is public on GitHub, so you can see exactly what changed, and say so when something breaks. roadmap-as-marketing

Frequently asked questions

InstructionsAnswer all questions.Open one to see the model answer.

1Is Brainfy free?[1]

Yes. Brainfy is free during beta — AI flashcards, spaced repetition, the AI tutor, the Pomodoro timer and the study planner are all unlocked at no cost, with no ads.

2What does Brainfy do?[1]

Brainfy turns your notes, PDFs and lecture videos into flashcards with AI, schedules reviews with spaced repetition, and helps you focus with a Pomodoro timer, a weekly planner and an AI tutor — all in one web app.

3Do I need an account to try Brainfy?[1]

No. You can try Brainfy instantly in your browser with no signup. Create a free account when you want your decks and progress synced across devices.

4Can I import decks from Anki or Quizlet?[1]

Yes. You can import Anki "Notes in Plain Text", Quizlet exports, or CSV files from any deck's Cards tab, and export your decks back to CSV anytime — no lock-in.

5How is Brainfy different from Anki or Quizlet?[1]

Brainfy combines Anki-style spaced repetition, Quizlet-style study modes (Learn, Match, Test), and AI that builds the cards from your notes — free and ad-free, in one place.

6Is my study data private?[1]

Yes. Your data syncs only to your own account, is never sold, and Brainfy shows no ads. See our Privacy Policy for details.
